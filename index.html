Who We Are

Just a Bunch of People Who Love Plays

The Play Reading Group is a casual monthly get-together for anyone who enjoys a good script. We're teachers, accountants, retirees, students and everyone in between.

We read, we stumble over lines, we laugh, and then we talk about the play over a pint. No pressure, no drama. Well, only the good kind.

  • 📜
    The Script. Our Curator picks the play carefully and shares it with members a few days before the session. New to the group? You'll be reading blind. Which is half the fun.
  • 🎲
    Rotating Roles. Everyone gets a part. Roles are assigned at random by our Host.
  • 🗣️
    Post-Read Discussion. After we read, we talk. Context, craft, opinions. All welcome.
  • 🍻
    Pub Setting. We meet at the Royal Windsor Pub, 610 Ford Drive in Oakville. Good pints and great plays go hand in hand.

A Typical Sunday

How a Session Works

01

Arrive & Settle In

Third Sunday of the month, 1:00 to 4:00 PM. Grab your drink, say hello, and claim a seat around the reading table.

→
02

Roles Assigned

The Curator shares the script a few days ahead so returning members can have a look. At the table, roles are handed out and we do a quick character rundown before we begin.

→
03

Read Together

We read through the full play together. Laughing, stumbling, discovering. Whether it's your first time or your fiftieth, something always surprises you.

→
04

Discuss & Debrief

Post-read, we open the floor. What surprised you? What landed? What didn't? The conversation is always lively.
